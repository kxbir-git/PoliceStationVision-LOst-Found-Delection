This portal is a public service for registering lost and unclaimed property. It does not constitute a First Information Report (FIR). For theft, robbery, or any criminal matter, please visit your nearest police station.

Your personal data is processed in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023. Data is used solely for lost property registration and matching, retained for 12 months, and you may request deletion at any time from your account.
