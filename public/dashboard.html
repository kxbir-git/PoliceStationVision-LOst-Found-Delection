DPDP Act, 2023 Compliance & Rights

As a citizen reporting to this Police Station portal, your personal data is protected under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023:

  • Data Minimization: Your national identity proofs (Aadhaar, PAN, DL, Voter ID) are never stored in full. Only the document type and masked last 4 digits (e.g. XXXX-XXXX-1234) are recorded.
  • Purpose Limitation: Collected contact and circumstance details are utilized strictly for lost-and-found reconciliation and legal verification.
  • Retention Policy: All registered records are retained for a statutory period of 12 months, after which they are marked for automatic archival and anonymization.
  • Right to Erasure / Deletion: You have the legal right to revoke consent and request deletion of your personal records from this system.

Request Immediate Data Deletion

Submitting a deletion request will immediately remove your reports from public listing, initiate a 30-day statutory grace window, and anonymize all personal identifiers across station registers in compliance with DPDP principles.
